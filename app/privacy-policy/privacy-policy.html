Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Brand Gallery ("we", "our", or "us") operates an online shopping and marketplace platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name

  • Shipping Address
  • Order Information
  • Device and Usage Information
  • Customer Support Communications

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process orders and deliveries
  • Provide customer support
  • Send order updates and notifications
  • Improve platform performance and security
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

3. Technology Providers

Neon Database

We use Neon to securely store application and customer data.

Cloudflare Workers

We use Cloudflare Workers to power backend APIs, authentication workflows, security services, and application logic.

UploadThing

We use UploadThing to store and manage product images, user-uploaded content, and media assets.

OneSignal

We use OneSignal to send order updates, account notifications, and important service announcements.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect customer information. Data is encrypted during transmission using HTTPS and protected through access controls and authentication systems.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary with:

  • Shipping and logistics providers
  • Service providers supporting platform operations
  • Government authorities when legally required

6. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account through the application or by contacting support.

Upon deletion, personal account information will be removed unless retention is required by law, accounting obligations, fraud prevention requirements, or dispute resolution purposes.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request a copy of your information
  • Object to certain processing activities where applicable

Location Services

Gallery may request one-time access to your device's GPS location when you choose to use location-based features such as address selection, delivery location confirmation, nearby services, or map assistance. We do not continuously track users, do not monitor their movements, and do not collect location data in the background. Location access is only used when explicitly requested by the user for a specific feature. Location data is used solely to provide the requested functionality and improve service accuracy. Once the requested action is completed, no ongoing location tracking occurs.
